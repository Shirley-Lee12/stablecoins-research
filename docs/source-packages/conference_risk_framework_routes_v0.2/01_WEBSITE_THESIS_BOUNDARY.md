# WEBSITE–THESIS RESEARCH BOUNDARY V0.2

## Website research source of truth

The only current research output represented as the Stablecoin Research Hub's own research is:

**Establishing a Multi-Dimensional Risk Framework over the Full Lifecycle of Stablecoins**

Conference presentation, October 2026.

## Allowed website research claims

The current study may claim that it:
- identifies and decomposes stablecoin risks into atomic risk units;
- organises risks into financial/institutional and crypto-native/DeFi modules;
- contains 55 atomic risks in the submitted framework;
- maps risks along a lifecycle dimension T0–T3;
- maps risks along an amplification/transmission dimension L0–L3;
- distinguishes triggers, structural vulnerabilities, dynamic amplification and cross-system outcomes;
- provides a conceptual foundation for future quantification and monitoring.

## Not allowed as current website research results

Do NOT import from the master's thesis:
- Transfer Entropy empirical results;
- conditional TE;
- Hawkes model results;
- TE–Hawkes comparison;
- thesis measurement nodes N01–N13 / E1–E2;
- systemic network-risk proxies;
- stress-test results;
- historical-event cases;
- thesis data pipeline status;
- thesis midterm results;
- thesis model-validation statistics.

Do NOT state that the website currently provides:
- a quantified risk index;
- live early warning;
- depeg probability;
- production monitoring;
- quantified transmission estimates;
- regulatory coverage scores.

## Future-work language

The conference study explicitly places quantification and monitoring after decomposition and mapping.

Use:
`Future research`

`currently monitoring`
unless later supported by a separate website research project.
