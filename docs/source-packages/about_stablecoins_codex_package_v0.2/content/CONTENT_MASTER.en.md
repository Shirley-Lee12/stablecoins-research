## `/about-stablecoins`
### Understand the mechanism before the label
A stablecoin is a transferable digital token designed to maintain a relatively stable value against a reference asset or unit. Stability is not an intrinsic property of the token; it depends on reserves, collateral, redemption, liquidation, hedging or other stabilisation arrangements.

Ask four questions:
1. What value is it trying to track?
2. What economically supports the claim and absorbs losses?
3. Who can mint, transfer and directly redeem?
4. What is supposed to restore the reference value after a deviation?

Stablecoins are distinct from CBDCs, tokenised bank deposits and tokenised fund shares.

## `/about-stablecoins/types`
### Mechanism before taxonomy
Classify through:
- economic backing;
- exit/redemption path;
- stabilisation mechanism.

Five teaching groups:
fiat-reserve-backed / crypto-collateralised / synthetic-hedged / algorithmic / hybrid & boundary cases.

Categories are not universally legal, permanent or mutually exclusive.

## `/about-stablecoins/regulatory-evolution`
### From risk perimeter to issuer, redemption and systemic oversight
Five stages:
risk perimeter → reserve/redemption obligations → international standards → dedicated issuer regimes → cross-border/systemic monitoring.

Do not duplicate `/regulatory`.

## Fiat-reserve-backed
Issuer + reserve + eligible direct redemption + secondary-market arbitrage.

Core distinction:
**Reserve backing does not mean every holder has identical direct issuer redemption access.**

Examples: USDC, USDT, PYUSD.

## Crypto-collateralised
Crypto collateral + overcollateralisation + liquidation + redemption/market arbitrage.

Contemporary teaching example: Liquity V2 BOLD.
Historical milestone: Maker's original Dai/Sai.

## Synthetic / hedged
Backing assets + derivatives hedge + custody/settlement infrastructure.

Example: Ethena USDe.

Core distinction:
synthetic/hedged is not the same as a classic endogenous algorithmic stablecoin.

## Algorithmic
Supply/incentive/arbitrage feedback with endogenous assets.

Canonical failure case: TerraUSD.

Core lesson:
reflexive backing can weaken precisely when confidence falls.

## Hybrid & boundary cases
Classify today's mechanism, not the project's historical reputation.

Current frxUSD is described by Frax as fiat-redeemable and fully collateralised with permitted cash-equivalent/tokenised-Treasury reserves.

Separate stablecoins from CBDCs, tokenised deposits, tokenised MMF/Treasury shares and yield-bearing wrappers.
