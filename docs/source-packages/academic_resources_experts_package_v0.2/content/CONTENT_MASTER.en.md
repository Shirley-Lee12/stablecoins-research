## `/academic-resources`

### Find the evidence behind stablecoin research questions

The Stablecoin Research Library brings together peer-reviewed papers, working papers, central-bank and institutional research, legal scholarship, primary regulatory sources, and data/methodology resources.

It is curated, not exhaustive.

Primary discovery should begin with research themes:
- Stability & arbitrage
- Runs & liquidity
- Reserves & transparency
- Primary/secondary markets & market structure
- Banking & monetary policy
- Systemic & macro-financial spillovers
- Regulation & law
- Payments & adoption
- Taxonomy & mechanism design
- Data, measurement & methodology

Search:
title / author / institution or venue / theme / keyword.

Filters:
theme / evidence type / year / language / access / author / institution.

Default sort:
year — newest first.

Each resource card shows:
title · authors · year · evidence type · venue/institution · themes · access · DOI/official source.

Do not reproduce long abstracts. Link to the original publication.

Evidence labels must remain visible: peer-reviewed research, working paper, institutional research, primary regulation, dataset/methodology and industry research are not interchangeable.

### Empty states

Successful query with no match:
`No approved public resources match the current filters.`

No approved records:
`No approved public resources are currently available.`

Directory/database failure:
`The public research directory is temporarily unavailable.`

A failed database query must not be rendered as a factual zero.

## `/experts`

### Trace stablecoin research through researchers and institutions

Public display title:
`Researchers & Institutions`

This is a directory of people whose directly relevant stablecoin research is represented in the public library. It is not a ranking of the world's “top stablecoin experts”.

A public profile requires:
- unambiguous identity;
- at least one verified directly stablecoin-focused output;
- authoritative publication/source record;
- verified current or publication-time affiliation;
- representative work linked to the research library.

Researcher cards:
name · role · institution · research focus · representative stablecoin research · last verified · official profile/source.

If only publication-time affiliation is verified, label it as such.

Researcher types may include:
Academic researcher / Legal-academic researcher / Central-bank researcher / Policy researcher / Institutional researcher.

These describe research context, not hierarchy.

Default sort:
recently verified.

Secondary sort:
name A–Z.

If a count is displayed, use:
`Resources in this library`

not:
`Total publications`.

Institutions are derived from verified public researcher/resource records. They are not ranked and must not be described as partners or leading institutions without separate evidence.

### Empty states

No matching public profile:
`No public researcher profiles match the current filters.`

Directory failure:
`The public researcher directory is temporarily unavailable.`

Do not turn a database failure into `0 experts`.
