# Researchers & Institutions Inclusion Policy V0.1

## Purpose

This directory is not a ranking of the “best stablecoin experts”.

It is a traceable directory of researchers whose directly relevant stablecoin work is represented in the public research library.

## Researcher inclusion

A public researcher profile may be shown when all are true:

1. Identity is unambiguous.
2. At least one directly stablecoin-focused public research output is verified.
3. The relevant output has an authoritative publication/source record.
4. Current affiliation or role is verified from:
   - official institutional profile; or
   - a current authoritative publication when no profile exists.
5. Representative work is linked to the resource library.

## Researcher types

Public labels may include:
- Academic researcher
- Legal / academic researcher
- Central-bank researcher
- Policy researcher
- Institutional researcher

These describe current research context, not hierarchy.

## Do not infer

Do not infer:
- seniority from citation count;
- “leading expert” status;
- political/regulatory endorsement;
- institutional endorsement of this website;
- expertise in every stablecoin topic from one paper.

## Affiliation date

Current affiliation should have:
`last_verified`.

Publication-time affiliation may differ from current affiliation.

If only publication-time affiliation is verified:
label it clearly and do not silently call it current.

## Institutions

Institution sections are aggregations of verified public records.

An institution appears because:
- a displayed researcher has a verified affiliation; and/or
- displayed resources are institutionally published.

Do not label institutions:
- top;
- leading;
- partner;
- sponsor;
- collaborator

unless separately supported.

## Sorting

Default:
- Recently verified; or
- Name A–Z.

Optional:
- publication year/activity within this library.

Do not default to:
- article count;
- citation count;
- “expert score”.

## Publication counts

If shown, label:
`Resources in this library`

Not:
`Total publications`

because the library is curated and incomplete.
