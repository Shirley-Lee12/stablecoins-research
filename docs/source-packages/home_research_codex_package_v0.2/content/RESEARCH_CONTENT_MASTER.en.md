## Completed conceptual research: 55 atomic risks
The AI-DEFIT 2026 research decomposes stablecoin risk into 55 conceptually distinct atomic risks and maps them across lifecycle position and amplification/transmission role.

**Boundary:** 55 is a conceptual taxonomy result, not a requirement to place 55 variables directly into one empirical model.
