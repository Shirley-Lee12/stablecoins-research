# Asia Stablecoin Development｜Content Master en V0.1
Status baseline: 2026-10-16.

## `/regional-developments`
### Asia is not following one stablecoin path.
Mainland China restricts private stablecoins while advancing e-CNY. Hong Kong has an active issuer-licensing regime and phased token launches. Singapore has live products under its existing PSA/DPT framework while the dedicated MAS-SCS regime is still being legislatively implemented. The UAE combines multiple regulatory perimeters rather than one national stablecoin licence.

## `/mainland-china`
### Mainland China is pursuing CBDC, not a private-stablecoin licensing model.
The 2026 multi-agency notice keeps virtual-currency business under a prohibition framework and explicitly addresses fiat-referenced stablecoins, offshore RMB-referenced issuance, offshore issuance by Mainland-controlled entities, and RWA tokenisation. e-CNY is CBDC/legal tender, not a stablecoin.

## `/hong-kong`
### Hong Kong has moved from legislation to licensing and phased distribution.
Anchorpoint FRS01 and HSBC FRS02 are active licences. HKDAP is live in institutional/corporate Beta Access; HSBC RedCoin was still not issued as of 30 September 2026.

## `/singapore`
### Stablecoins are live, but the dedicated MAS-SCS regime is still completing legislative implementation.
MAS finalised policy design in 2023 and opened a PS Act amendments consultation on 1 September 2026. XSGD/XUSD are live under the existing PSA/DPT/MPI environment. Do not label them MAS-SCS regulated stablecoins.

## `/uae`
### The UAE is a multi-perimeter stablecoin market.
CBUAE governs onshore Payment Tokens; VARA governs Dubai virtual assets excluding DIFC; ADGM/FSRA has an FRT regime; DIFC/DFSA has a separate Crypto Token framework. AE Coin and Zand AED are live AED products under CBUAE-licensed issuers.
