# Current `/regulatory` Audit V0.1

Reference: website export dated 2026-10-15.
Audit baseline: 2026-10-16.

## What is already good

The existing page already:
- compares eight jurisdictions;
- separates regulatory scope, issuer permission, reserves, redemption and ongoing supervision;
- distinguishes domestic regimes from international standards;
- uses a comparison table rather than decorative country cards;
- deep-links Asian jurisdictions.

The page therefore needs a **targeted factual/status rebuild**, not a completely different concept.

## P0 factual/status corrections

### United States
Old copy: `联邦框架已立法 / 已生效`

Correction:
- GENIUS Act was enacted on 18 Jul 2025.
- The Act has a delayed effective date: earlier of 18 months after enactment or 120 days after primary federal regulators issue final implementing rules.
- Federal rulemaking remains underway as of 16 Oct 2026.
- Current federal sources expect 18 Jan 2027 absent an earlier trigger.

Public status:
`ENACTED — IMPLEMENTATION RULEMAKING — NOT YET EFFECTIVE`

### United Kingdom
Old copy: `政策已确定；最终规则待发布`

Correction:
- FSMA 2000 (Cryptoassets) Regulations 2026 were made on 4 Feb 2026.
- FCA published final stablecoin rules/guidance on 30 Jun 2026.
- Full crypto-regime commencement is 25 Oct 2027.
- Bank of England systemic-stablecoin rules remain a distinct prudential/systemic layer.

Public status:
`FINAL RULES PUBLISHED — FULL COMMENCEMENT 25 OCT 2027`

### Singapore
Old copy can visually imply the 2023 finalised framework is already a binding dedicated issuer regime.

Correction:
- MAS finalised policy design in Aug 2023.
- On 1 Sep 2026 MAS opened consultation on legislative amendments to the Payment Services Act to implement the MAS-SCS framework.
- Consultation closes 16 Oct 2026.
- MAS itself refers to regulated stablecoin issuer licensees as future entities after the framework comes into force.

Public status:
`IMPLEMENTATION CONSULTATION — NOT YET IN FORCE`

### Mainland China
Old copy referenced a 2025 reaffirmation.

Correction:
- Current baseline is the eight-agency Notice 银发〔2026〕42号 dated 6 Feb 2026.
- It expressly addresses stablecoins and replaces the 2021 notice.
- Virtual-currency related business remains illegal financial activity.
- Offshore RMB-linked stablecoin issuance requires relevant approval.
- Mainland entities and controlled overseas entities may not issue virtual currency offshore without approval.

Public status:
`RESTRICTIVE / PROHIBITIVE BOUNDARY — CURRENT 2026 NOTICE`

## Other current-status refinements

### Hong Kong
Regime in force since 1 Aug 2025.
HKMA register currently lists two licensed issuers effective 10 Apr 2026.
Do not equate issuer licence with a token already being publicly launched.

### EU
MiCA ART/EMT regime remains applicable.
A 2026 MiCA review/consultation does not suspend the current rules.

### Japan
Electronic Payment Instrument regime is in force.
2026 amendments added a new intermediary regime from 1 Jun 2026 and updated related requirements.
Use the legal category `electronic payment instruments`, not a generic “Japan stablecoin licence”.

### UAE
CBUAE Payment Token Services Regulation C2/2024 is in force from 31 Aug 2024.
Scope includes issuance, conversion, custody/transfer.
The UAE is multi-perimeter; the global table must label this row `CBUAE onshore perimeter` rather than implying one UAE-wide regulator.
