## Page role

Keep `/regulatory` focused on:
`current legal status + cross-jurisdiction comparison`.

Do not repeat:
- About Stablecoins regulatory-evolution narrative;
- full Asia product/regulatory pages;
- market/data content.

## Required headline logic

Chinese:
`稳定币监管：比较的是法律状态，不只是政策态度`

English:
`Stablecoin regulation: compare legal status, not just policy direction`

## Status wording — frozen

### United States
Use:
`Enacted — implementation rulemaking — not yet effective`

Do not use:
- `In force`
- `US stablecoin regime effective since 2025`

### European Union
Use:
`MiCA in force / applicable`

2026 review does not replace or pause current MiCA.

### United Kingdom
Use:
`Final rules published — full commencement 25 October 2027`

Do not use:
- `final rules pending`
- `fully in force`

### Hong Kong
Use:
`Issuer regime in force — licensed issuers exist`

Keep licence and token launch separate.

### Singapore
Use:
`Implementation consultation — dedicated MAS-SCS regime not yet in force`

Do not use:
- `MAS-regulated stablecoin framework is already live`
- `XSGD/XUSD are MAS-SCS regulated stablecoins`

unless a future official source establishes that status.

### Japan
Use:
`Electronic Payment Instrument regime in force`

Use the statutory category rather than inventing one generic stablecoin licence.

### UAE
Use:
`CBUAE onshore Payment Token Services Regulation in force`

Do not present this one row as the whole UAE regulatory system.

### Mainland China
Use:
`Restrictive / prohibitive boundary`

Current notice:
银发〔2026〕42号.

Do not use the superseded 2021 notice as the current primary baseline.

## Legal-language boundaries

Avoid:
- `approved stablecoin` when source only proves issuer licence;
- `regulated product` when source only proves an entity licence;
- `legal` / `illegal` without the correct scoped activity;
- `audit` when source says attestation/examination;
- `global law` for FSB/FATF/CPMI-IOSCO standards.

## No ranking language

Do not write:
- most friendly
- most advanced
- safest regime
- strictest regime
- best jurisdiction

unless a clearly defined external ranking is being discussed. V0.2 includes no such ranking.

## Dates

Use absolute dates for current regulatory status.
Avoid relative labels such as:
- recently
- this year
- now

where a concrete date is available.

## English

Use:
- authorisation
- finalised
- recognised
- stabilisation

Retain official US document spelling inside titles when necessary.

## Disclaimer

Keep a concise statement:
`This page is for comparative research and is not legal advice.`

Do not add long legal boilerplate.
